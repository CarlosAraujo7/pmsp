\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Instances and budgets}
The identical-machine experiments use twelve synthetic classes $(n,m)\in\{(20,4),\ldots,(300,20)\}$ with $100$ independently generated instances each (seeds fixed per instance), $6{,}780$
published instances (B, C, E, F; stratified sample), $4{,}160$ held-out and $2{,}690$ fresh published instances for confirmatory tests, and a certified lower bound for the synthetic ones.
The standard budget is $T=1{,}000$ iterations; larger budgets and CPU-equalised budgets are stated where used. Each run is single-threaded; all code, seeds and result files are in the repository.

\paragraph{Pre-specification}
Every confirmatory test belongs to a family, fixed with its hypothesis, decision rule and code hash before the data (Section~\ref{sec:protocol}); the full list, with verdicts, is in
Supplementary Table~\ref{S-tab:s_prereg_all}. Exploratory analyses are labelled as such, and the cases in which the predictions recorded beforehand were wrong are reported.

\paragraph{Reading the tables}
$\bar d$ is the mean paired difference in the makespan in per cent (negative: the first method better); W/L/T counts wins, losses and ties; $p_{\mathrm{Holm}}$ is Holm-adjusted within the family.
