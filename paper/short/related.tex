\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Adaptive operator selection and reinforcement learning}
Adaptive operator selection (AOS) estimates, during the search, which operator or strategy is worth applying \citep{FialhoEtal2010,BurkeEtal2013}. Classical mechanisms include uniform
random selection, the adaptive weights of ALNS \citep{PisingerRopke2007}, bandits such as UCB \citep{AuerEtal2002} and supervised predictors \citep{MaturanaSaubion2008}; a survey
of bandit-based AOS found that simple exploration--exploitation rules often beat more complex methods in small operator spaces \citep{FialhoEtal2010}. Reinforcement learning adds a state
that conditions the choice \citep{BengioBengio2021}; \citet{CalvetEtal2017} coined \emph{learnheuristic} for metaheuristics coupled to a learning component. Tabular Q-Learning as an operator
selector has been explored for permutation flowshop inside Iterated Greedy \citep{KarimiMamaghanEtal2023,KarimiMamaghanEtal2024}, for makespan in additive manufacturing
\citep{AlicastroEtal2021} and, closest to this work, for the destruction size of an Iterated Greedy for identical parallel machines with setups and weighted earliness/tardiness
\citep{MissaouiOSullivan2026}, building on the adaptive destruction of \citet{MissaouiRuiz2022}. Those studies report that the learning-guided method beats baselines; the question of how much
of the gain belongs to the policy, as opposed to the search structure, the budget and the tuning, is the object of the present article.

\paragraph{Parallel machine scheduling}
P$||C_{\max}$ is NP-hard \citep{GareyJohnson1979}. The classical constructive heuristics are LPT \citep{Graham1969}, MULTIFIT \citep{CoffmanGareyJohnson1978} and the
differencing method LDM \citep{KarmarkarKarp1983,MichielsEtal2007}; local search with multi-exchange neighbourhoods \citep{FrangioniEtal2004} and simulated annealing \citep{LiPerkins2024}
and bee-colony \citep{DavidovicEtal2012} metaheuristics have been proposed, but exact bin-packing-based and arc-flow formulations
\citep{DellAmicoEtal2008,MradSouayah2018,GharbiBamatraf2022,AkramEtal2024} solve most published benchmark instances in well under a second, the hard ones remaining costly
\citep{OstojicEtAl2025}. The linear relaxation of the arc-flow model is a cheap, certified lower bound, which this article uses to tell near-optimal from provably optimal solutions. For
unrelated machines (R$||C_{\max}$), Iterated Greedy with local search is the established heuristic \citep{FanjulPeyroRuiz2010}, linear programming gives a 2-approximation by rounding \citep{LenstraEtal1990}, and a recent exact algorithm proves optimality for about two thirds of the instances of the standard benchmark within 300 seconds \citep{AbladEtAl2021}.

\paragraph{Evaluation methodology and offline configuration}
That the component responsible for a gain must be isolated by controlled experiments, and not by a competitive comparison of whole algorithms, is a long-standing position in the heuristics literature
\citep{Hooker1995,BarrEtal1995}; a similar concern with confounded comparisons, whose results depend on implementation details, hyperparameters
and budgets, arose in deep reinforcement learning \citep{Henderson2018}. For parameters, automatic offline configuration on training instances (ParamILS, iterated racing;
\citealp{HutterEtal2009,LopezIbanezEtal2016}) is the standard alternative to adapting them during the run, which has its own taxonomy and surveys \citep{EibenEtal1999,KarafotiasEtal2015,AletiMoser2016}. The protocol of this article
brings these principles to learnheuristics: a random control, an offline-validated competitor, an equal-CPU budget and analyses specified before the data. How far current practice is from them was checked on 12 recent open-access papers that embed a learned selector in a metaheuristic
(Supplementary Section~\ref{S-sec:m-litaudit}): five include a random-selection control and eight an ablation of the policy, but only two compare with the variants without learning at an equal time limit, two include a static configuration tuned offline, and one of the six that use a statistical test corrects for multiple comparisons; all twelve conclude that the learned component helps.

\paragraph{Iterated Greedy}
\citet{RuizStutzle2007} introduced Iterated Greedy for the permutation flowshop, showing that a destruction--reconstruction cycle is competitive with far more complex metaheuristics; its
destruction size is a sensitive parameter that usually requires instance-specific tuning. None of the studies above isolates, with controls at equal CPU time and offline-tuned baselines, what an
online learner adds to the search it controls.
